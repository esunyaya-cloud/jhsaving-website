Our Story

Integrated Household Storage Solutions Provider

Jinhua Saving Industry and Trade Co. Ltd was established in 2017 and is a professional manufacturer specializing in the R&D, production, and export of household storage products. Located in Jinhua, Zhejiang Province, China, the company operates a modern manufacturing facility of over 5,000 square meters, integrating cutting, sewing, assembly, and quality inspection into a complete production system.

With more than 120 skilled employees and over 100 advanced machines, including flat sewing machines, synchronized machines, high-post machines, automated sewing systems, and precision cutting equipment, Saving Industry maintains strong production efficiency and stable quality control. Our annual output exceeds 3 million units, enabling us to handle both customized small-batch orders and large-scale mass production.

How We Work

From Inquiry to Delivery

A streamlined process designed for global B2B customers — clear communication, on-time delivery, zero surprises.

1️⃣

Inquiry & Consultation

Share your requirements — material, size, color, logo, quantity. We provide professional advice and competitive pricing within 24 hours.

2️⃣

Sample Development

We create samples based on your specifications. Free sample kits available for standard products. Custom samples typically take 7-10 days.

3️⃣

Sample Approval

Review the sample, request modifications if needed, and confirm final specifications. We only proceed after your written approval.

4️⃣

Mass Production

After 30% deposit, production begins. Lead time is typically 20-35 days depending on order volume. We send production updates regularly.

5️⃣

Quality Inspection

Multi-point QC checks during and after production. Pre-shipment inspection report and photos provided. Third-party inspection welcomed.

6️⃣

Packaging & Shipping

Polybags + export cartons as standard. Customized packaging available. We arrange sea/air/express shipping per your preference and pay 70% balance.
