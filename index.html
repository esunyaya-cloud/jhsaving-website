About SAVING

Integrated Household Storage Solutions Provider

Jinhua Saving Industry and Trade Co. Ltd was established in 2017 and is a professional manufacturer specializing in the R&D, production, and export of household storage products. Located in Jinhua, Zhejiang Province, China, we operate a modern manufacturing facility of over 5,000 square meters.

With more than 120 skilled employees and over 100 advanced machines, we maintain strong production efficiency and stable quality control — delivering over 3 million units annually to customers worldwide.

  • Trusted long-term partner of MINISO, Disney, Walmart, Amazon & BM
  • Exported to 30+ countries across Japan, USA, Europe, Americas, and Middle East
  • Complete OEM/ODM services from samples to mass production
  • Committed to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction
